### My research

My research focuses on the union of physics and computing, particularly within the captivating domain of quantum computing. I have dedicated myself to exploring the Fast quasiadiabatic dynamics (FAQUAD) protocol, a promising innovation within the realm of quantum computing. This protocol has been instrumental to my research efforts, shaping my exploration as I delve deeper into it.

A significant aspect of my research involves conducting simulations to assess the effectiveness of FAQUAD across various quantum computing scenarios. Through these simulations, I have been able to compare FAQUAD's performance with alternative protocols, gaining insights into its strengths, weaknesses, and possible areas for enhancement.

### Biography

I graduated in 2025 with a Bachelor's degree in Physics with honors in Introduction to Financial Markets and Operations, and Econophysics at the University of the Balearic Islands ([UIB](https://www.uib.eu/)). My academic journey has been marked by a keen interest in the intersection of physics and economics, exploring the dynamics of complex systems and their applications in financial markets.

Currently, I am pursuing a Master's degree in Complex Systems Physics offered by the Institute for Cross-Disciplinary Physics and Complex Systems ([IFISC](https://ifisc.uib-csic.es/en/)) (CSIC-UIB). My research interests are focused on the fascinating field of nanotechnology, particularly on qubits and qudits, as potential elements in the development of quantum computing.
